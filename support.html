Quick fixes

  1. Restart the game. Close the app completely (swipe it away from the recent apps list) and open it again.
  2. No sound? Check that the speaker button in the game is ON and the device volume is up. On iPhone/iPad, also check the silent / mute switch (or Silent Mode in Control Center) - the game follows it.
  3. No dino voice? Open Settings in the game and make sure Dino Voice is ON and Dino Talk is not OFF.
  4. The game paused by itself. That is on purpose: it pauses whenever you leave the app, lock the screen or get a call, so you never lose a run. Tap RESUME.
  5. Screen looks too small or rotated. Primal Dash is made for landscape - turn your phone sideways.
  6. Still not working? Uninstall and reinstall the app from the App Store or Google Play. Note: this resets your high score, because it is only stored on your device.
  7. Contact support at adnanmoon@gmail.com - we are happy to help.

Common questions

Do I need the internet? No. The game works completely offline.

Does the game listen to my child? No. The dinosaur only plays recordings that are built into the app. The game never uses the microphone.

Are there ads or purchases? No ads and no purchases in this version.

Where is my high score saved? Only on your device. See the privacy policy.

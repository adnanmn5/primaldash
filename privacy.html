Privacy Policy - Primal Dash: Dino Run

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Primal Dash: Dino Run ("the game") is made by Adnan Yawar, an individual developer. This policy explains what happens to information when you play the game on Android, iPhone or iPad, or play the desktop version.

Summary

  • No account or login is required.
  • The game does not collect personal information.
  • There are no analytics, tracking or advertising tools (SDKs) in the game.
  • The game does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • The talking dinosaur only plays recordings that are packaged inside the app. The game never listens to you and never records audio.
  • Your high score, Adventure progress (stars, completed levels, creatures, fossils), chosen creature and colors and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game is designed to work completely offline.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress and choices, the game saves a small amount of information locally on your device: your Endless Run high score, your Adventure progress (completed levels, stars, best scores, unlocked creatures and colors, fossils found, moves learned and Creature Book pages seen), the creature and colors you chose and your settings (Sound, Dino Voice, Dino Talk level and, on iPhone/iPad, Vibration). This information never leaves your device through the game and is not sent to the developer or anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the game (or clear the browser data for the desktop version).

If your device makes its own backups (for example iCloud Backup or Android device backup), the operating system may include this saved game data in your personal backup. Those backups are controlled by your device settings and the provider of your device, not by the game.

The talking dinosaur

The dinosaur speaks short English phrases. These voice clips were created in advance and are included inside the app. When the dinosaur talks, the app simply plays one of these clips and shows the same words on screen. The game does not use speech recognition, does not request microphone access, does not record anyone and does not send any audio anywhere.

Internet and permissions

The game does not need an internet connection and does not send or receive data over the internet. It does not request any permission that shows a prompt. On Android, the app includes the standard "internet" permission used by the app framework to display the game's own built-in files; the game itself makes no network requests. Optional vibration on iPhone/iPad uses the built-in haptics of your device and requires no permission.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

This version of the game contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services and no social features. No information is shared with or sold to third parties.

Like every app, the game is downloaded through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google may collect information about downloads and app usage under their own privacy policies; that collection is done by the store or operating system, not by the game.

Children

The game is suitable for children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There is no chat, no account, no user-generated content and no way to share information with others inside the game.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle information differently (for example online leaderboards), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "Effective date" above will change.

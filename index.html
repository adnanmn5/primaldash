PRIMAL DASH
DINO RUN

A colorful prehistoric runner with a full Adventure Mode - and the classic Endless Run - where dinosaurs, storms, erupting volcanoes and changing worlds make every run different.

Adventure Mode5 worlds, 25 levels and 5 friendly bosses. Hearts, stars, a world map and 7 colors to unlock.
8 prehistoric creaturesFrom T. rex to Triceratops and the Woolly rhino - each with its own power move. A Creature Book with names to hear and fossils to find.
Endless RunThe classic game: run as far as you can while the world speeds up. Beat your high score.
Run, jump & duckTap to jump, tap again for a double jump, hold to duck under pterosaurs or smash through logs, plants and snowmen.
A living worldJungle, desert, snow, volcanoes and a moonlit swamp - with rain, snow, thunderstorms and day and night.
A talking dinosaurShort, simple English phrases and creature names with matching speech bubbles. No microphone, ever.
Play anywhereWorks offline. No account, no ads, no data collected. Progress stays on your device.
